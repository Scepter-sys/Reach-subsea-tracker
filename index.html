A working-status board for Reach Subsea ASA's chartered and owned vessels, built from public AIS navigation data. Navigation status is the closest public proxy for "working" — a vessel underway or on DP at a field is almost certainly on a job; one that's moored or at anchor in port is likely idle or in transit between contracts.

How to read this. Each card links to that vessel's live public AIS page (VesselFinder). Open the link for real-time position, speed and course — this page shows a manually-verified snapshot rather than a live feed, since AIS data providers don't allow direct embedding without a paid API key or a map widget. All links go to confirmed IMO-matched pages except Offshore Surveyor and Reach Remote 2, where I couldn't verify an exact IMO/MMSI — those two open a name search on VesselFinder instead of a guessed page, so the link works but you'll need to pick the right result.

Limitations. "Underway" means the vessel is moving or holding position under power (e.g. DP survey work) — it's a strong signal of an active job, not proof of one. A vessel can be moored between contracts, in transit to a new field, or under maintenance while still showing "underway" briefly, or vice-versa. Cross-check against Reach Subsea's own charter announcements for contract-level confirmation.
